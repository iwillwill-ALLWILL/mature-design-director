# Document and Editorial Capability

Use this capability for handbooks, reports, worksheets, proposals, guides, long-form PDFs, and other page-based artifacts whose success depends on reading flow rather than slide performance. The umbrella owns audience, voice, art direction, truth, and approval; document/PDF tools own file mechanics.

## Capability boundary

This capability owns:

- reading sequence and editorial hierarchy;
- page architecture, navigation, and information scent;
- typography, tables, figures, callouts, captions, and references as one reading system;
- digital/print behavior and rendered-page QA.

It does not own source facts, legal review, Word/PDF internals, OCR engines, or publication authorization. Use the presentation capability when the artifact is paced for live or slide-by-slide viewing; use visual explanation when a diagram carries the argument.

## Reading contract

Before authoring, define:

- reader, context, prior knowledge, and decision or action after reading;
- canonical sources and claims that require citation;
- reading mode: linear, reference, worksheet, printable, mobile PDF, or mixed;
- required sections, navigation, length, editability, accessibility, and print constraints;
- what can be summarized, what must remain exact, and what is private.

Create an outline in encounter order. Each section must answer one reader question and earn its visual weight. Move production notes, internal IDs, model/provider details, TODOs, and raw schemas out of the visible document.

## Editorial system

Establish a semantic system before styling pages:

- title, section, subsection, body, caption, label, quote, code/data, and footnote roles;
- page grid, margins, line length, baseline rhythm, spacing hierarchy, and running furniture;
- color roles with grayscale/print fallback;
- figure, table, callout, and evidence conventions;
- navigation: contents, page numbers, cross-references, bookmarks, or index when useful.

Do not decorate prose with repeated cards. Use containers only when they communicate a distinct content role. Prefer a small number of strong page archetypes over unique layouts on every page.

## Production loop

1. Build the content/source map and resolve contradictions.
2. Draft the hierarchy with real content, not lorem ipsum.
3. Prove the cover/opening, densest page, longest table/figure page, and closing/action page.
4. Choose one authoring source that can reproduce the final artifact.
5. Produce complete pages with captions, references, links, and required metadata.
6. Render every page, inspect a contact sheet, then inspect full-size risk pages.
7. Fix the source and rerender; never patch only the exported PDF unless the task is explicitly a PDF repair.

## Figures, tables, and evidence

- Give every figure a job and a caption that states what to notice.
- Keep tables for comparison or exact lookup; convert narrative tables into prose or diagrams.
- Preserve units, denominators, date/timezone, uncertainty, and source scope.
- Never recreate product text or results and present them as captured evidence.
- Generated imagery may set tone but must not impersonate a real person, deployment, UI, measurement, or certificate.

## Digital, print, and accessibility

Verify as applicable:

- selectable/searchable text, logical reading order, headings, alt text, link purpose, language metadata, contrast, and keyboard navigation;
- embedded/substituted fonts, missing glyphs, overflow, widows/orphans, table splits, figure resolution, crop/bleed, color profile, and grayscale legibility;
- phone-scale reading for mobile PDFs and actual printer proof for print-critical work;
- file size, bookmarks, links, metadata, attachments, and prohibited active content.

## Evidence of completion

A document is complete only when:

- every required section exists and the claims trace to canonical sources;
- the complete rendered document has been inspected in reading order;
- the densest and most fragile pages pass full-size review;
- source and exported artifact are fresh and reproducible;
- accessibility/print checks match the delivery context;
- the exact delivered binary and publication state are reported separately.
